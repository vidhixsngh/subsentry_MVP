- **Product Name:** Subsentry — subscription tracker

- **Core Problem:** Minimizes payment delays and financial anxiety by removing the need for manual follow-ups.

- **Font Style:** Inter Sans or similar (modern, rounded UI)
- **UI Style:** Clean card layouts, rounded corners, gentle shadows, high accessibility contrast
- **Iconography:** Lucide or Material (clean, flat style)
- **Typography:** Modern sans-serif (Inter, SF Pro, Manrope); consistent typographic hierarchy (H1, H2, paragraph); minimum 14px font size
- **Layout:** Responsive (web and mobile), modern minimalist approach with ample whitespace, soft shadows, left-aligned text, and primary CTAs right-aligned
